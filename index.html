TRADE MEDS: Stylish & Functional Medical Scrubs, Lab Coats, and Stethoscopes for Men & Women!

Introduction:

TRADE MEDS is your premier destination for top-quality medical scrubs, consultant lab coats, and underscrubs for healthcare professionals across India. We believe every doctor, surgeon, nurse, and medical student deserves comfortable, flexible, and breathable medical apparel that also projects an impeccably professional clinical image.

Medical Scrub Suits for Men and Women

Experience the perfect fusion of functionality and contemporary medical fashion. Crafted from our signature polyester-viscose and ecoflex™ blends, our scrubs offer superior comfort, fluid-barrier protection, and practical utility for high-stress hospital routines.

Types of Scrubs for Healthcare Heroes:

  • V-Neck Scrubs: Clean, modern, flattering V-neck cut tailored with reinforced stitching and pen pockets.
  • Mandarin Collar Scrubs: Sophisticated standing collar scrubs for senior consultants and clinical directors.
  • ecoflex™ 4-Way Stretch Scrubs: Created from recycled PET bottles with moisture-wicking and fluid-repellent finish.
  • Full-Sleeve Scrubs: Maximum coverage and barrier protection for critical ICU and infectious disease wards.

Lab Coat Aprons for Doctors and Consultants

Our lab coats blend distinguished authority with practical storage. Made from high-density stain-resistant poly-cotton twill with dedicated iPad pockets, interior phone holders, and stethoscope loops.

TRADE MEDS 6sense Stethoscopes

Building on our mission to provide complete solutions for medical professionals, the 6sense Stethoscope features aerospace-grade acoustic diaphragms for exceptional auscultation clarity, backed by a 30-day trial and 5-year replacement warranty.
